01 / At your desk

A page that
gets out of
your way.

Open the menubar. Write the thought. Return to your day. Tasks, time blocks, and your journal share one quietly editable page.

  1. Write directly on the page.Markdown reveals itself on the line you’re editing. Move away and the page settles back into place.
  2. Give the day a shape.Drag, move, and resize time blocks. Every change becomes a line in the same Markdown file.
  3. Keep the thread.Carry unfinished tasks forward, start a timestamped journal entry, and leave a word about your mood.
